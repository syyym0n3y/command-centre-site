Command Centre

A private tool for running a small group of YouTube channels.

What it does

Command Centre handles the routine work of operating YouTube channels that we own: uploading finished videos, keeping titles and descriptions current, and reading back how each video performed so we can decide what to make next. It is used by one operator on channels that operator controls. It is not a product, there are no accounts to sign up for, and nobody else's channels are involved.

How it connects to YouTube

Each channel is connected once through Google's standard consent screen. We ask for four permissions and use all four:

  • youtube.upload — to publish a finished video to the channel it was made for.
  • youtube.force-ssl — to update a video's title, description and thumbnail after upload.
  • youtube.readonly — to confirm which channel a connection belongs to, so a video is never sent to the wrong one.
  • yt-analytics.readonly — to read views, watch time and retention for our own videos.

Uploads are made privately by default. A person decides whether anything goes public.

Where the data lives

On hardware we control, in a database that does not leave it. There is no third-party analytics service, no advertising network, and no data sharing of any kind. The privacy policy sets out the detail.

YouTube API Services

This application uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel you are also agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's own handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can review or remove this application's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
