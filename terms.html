What this is

Command Centre is a private application operated by Ona Mejelo for managing YouTube channels that we own. It is not offered to the public, sold, or licensed. There is no sign-up, no subscription and no fee.

Who may use it

The operator, and anyone the operator has directly authorised to work on these channels. Connecting a channel requires going through Google's consent screen with an account that controls that channel.

What it will do with a connected channel

  • Upload videos, set to private unless a person chooses otherwise.
  • Update the title, description, tags and thumbnail of videos it created.
  • Read performance figures for those videos.

It will not delete a channel, change channel-level settings it was not asked to change, post comments, or act on any channel other than the ones explicitly connected.

Content published through this application must also meet YouTube's Community Guidelines and monetisation policies. Videos that use synthetic or AI-assisted media are disclosed to YouTube as required.

Ending access

Either side can end this at any moment. Remove the application at myaccount.google.com/permissions and it loses access immediately. We may also disconnect a channel at any time.
